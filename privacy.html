Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

QuestionPaper (https://papermate.streamlit.app) is run by K Shanmugapriya, an individual based in Chennai, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who else handles it and what you can ask us to do. It follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and the username you choose (accounts made before 18 November 2026 may also hold the school or tuition centre that was given at sign-up). Your password is stored only in a one-way scrambled form, so nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Plan details: your free trial or plan, its end date, and any credits.
  • Usage records: the time of each AI request, the kind of document made (for example "question paper"), and its AI cost. These are linked to your username and are used for daily limits, billing and running costs.
  • AI-generated question papers and MCQ papers: the papers the AI makes for you, with the class, subject, topic and settings you chose. We keep them to improve the app and to reuse questions in papers for other users, without your name, so that AI costs stay low.
  • Feedback: the 👍 / 👎 and comments you choose to send.
  • Payment records: the plan bought, the amount, the date, and Razorpay's payment reference. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay handles those.

What we do not keep

  • Your other documents. Answer keys, worksheets and explanations are not saved. No document can be opened again in the app after you log out or close the page, so please download what you need.
  • Your uploaded or pasted papers. Photos, PDFs and text you give on the Answer Key page are sent to the AI to be read, and are never saved or reused by us.

Who else handles your data

We use these services to run QuestionPaper. Each handles data only for that purpose: - OpenAI: the AI that writes the documents. Your topic, instructions and any uploaded pages are sent to OpenAI. OpenAI says it does not use API data to train its models by default. Please don't put students' personal details in what you type or upload. - Google: account, plan, usage and feedback records and AI-generated question papers and MCQ papers are kept in a private Google Sheet. Sign-up and password codes are emailed through Gmail. - Streamlit (Snowflake Inc.): hosts the app. Its servers may be outside India. - Razorpay: processes payments under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

Cookies

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The hosting platform (Streamlit) may set the cookies it needs to run the app.

How long we keep it

  • Account, plan and feedback details: until you delete your account.
  • Payment records: as long as Indian tax and accounting laws require (generally up to 8 years), even after you delete your account.
  • Usage records: kept for cost and billing records, linked only to a username.
  • AI-generated question papers and MCQ papers: kept so their questions can be reused, linked only to a username and never shown with your name.

When you delete your account, we also keep a one-way scrambled fingerprint of your email address. It cannot be turned back into your email. It is used only to stop the free trial being claimed again.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to questionpapercbse.app@gmail.com. You can delete your account yourself at any time (see Delete Your Account).

Grievance contact: K Shanmugapriya, questionpapercbse.app@gmail.com. We acknowledge every request within 7 days and resolve it within 30 days.

Who it is for

QuestionPaper is for teachers, tutors and parents. It is also for students who want to practise, study on their own and boost their academic results. Students under 18 should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission. Ideally, the parent or guardian creates the account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. For a significant change, we will also tell you in the app.
