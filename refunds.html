Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

All QuestionPaper plans are one-time payments. Nothing renews automatically, so there is no subscription to cancel. A plan simply ends on its end date.

When you get a full refund

  • Monthly or Yearly plan: ask within 7 days of paying, having used 2 or fewer generations from that plan.
  • Single paper or Pack of 10 (credits): ask before any of its credits are used.
  • Money was debited but the plan didn't reach your account: we either activate the plan or give a full refund, whichever you prefer.
  • We charged you twice for the same plan: the extra payment is refunded in full.

When we can't give a refund

  • After 7 days, or once more than 2 generations of a Monthly or Yearly plan have been used.
  • Once Single paper or Pack of 10 credits have been used.
  • For days or credits left unused when a plan ends.

How to ask for a refund

Email questionpapercbse.app@gmail.com from the email address on your account. Include your username and the payment reference from Razorpay's receipt. We reply within 2 working days.

When the money reaches you

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. They normally arrive within 5-7 working days, depending on your bank. If a payment fails but money is debited, your bank usually reverses it by itself within 5-7 working days. If it doesn't, write to us.
